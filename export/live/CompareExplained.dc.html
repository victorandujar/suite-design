Decision summary

High spread adds €1.38M of annual revenue and 0.6 pt of IRR on the same asset — same 65.2 GWh, same €42.1M. 4 h variant discharges 27.2 GWh more on the same market for €8.6M of additional CAPEX, worth €1.93M a year. 4 h variant + High spread moves both dimensions at once: €3.63M more revenue and 1.3 pt of IRR, on €8.6M more CAPEX. 4 h variant + High spread gives the highest return at 14.1%, while Base 2 h + High spread earns the most per MWh discharged at €150.3. Extra duration buys volume, not price.
